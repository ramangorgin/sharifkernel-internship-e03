01 / THE OPPORTUNITY

Language should never be
left behind.

Artificial intelligence is changing how people learn, communicate, and access knowledge. But these advances are not equally available to every language.

For Sorani Kurdish, useful linguistic knowledge can be scattered across books, documents, and individual expertise. Kurdish AI imagines bringing that knowledge together — making it more structured, searchable, and useful for people and technology.

The starting point: turn fragmented language resources into a connected foundation for future language tools.

03 / THE TECHNOLOGY

Built on language.
Powered by intelligence.

The vision is not simply to place a chatbot on top of a language. It is to build the language foundation that makes useful AI experiences possible.

NLP

Natural Language Processing

Explore words, morphology, grammar, and linguistic structure.

ML

Machine Learning

Identify patterns, classify language data, and support adaptive learning.

RAG

Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Connect language models to curated, verifiable linguistic knowledge.

LLM

Large Language Models

Enable natural interaction, explanations, and learning experiences.

VEC

Find related concepts by meaning, not only exact word matches.
